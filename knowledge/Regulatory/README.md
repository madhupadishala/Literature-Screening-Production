# Regulatory Knowledge

This area stores authoritative regulatory/guidance source documents by authority and framework.

## Major collections

- `EMA/GVP/` — EMA/HMA Good Pharmacovigilance Practices modules, addenda, annexes and population/product considerations.
- `ICH/E2-Safety/` — ICH E2 pharmacovigilance/safety guideline family.
- `ICH/E6-GCP/` — ICH Good Clinical Practice where clinical safety/PV processes depend on GCP.
- `FDA/21-CFR/` — applicable US Code of Federal Regulations provisions for safety reporting, electronic records/signatures and regulated clinical/postmarketing processes.
- `FDA/Guidance/` — FDA safety/PV guidance documents.
- `Privacy/HIPAA/` — applicable US HIPAA privacy/security materials when protected health information is processed.
- `Privacy/GDPR/` — applicable EU/EEA privacy/data-protection sources.
- `GxP/` — cross-cutting GxP/computerised-system/data-integrity guidance relevant to PV systems.
- jurisdiction folders for CDSCO, MHRA, Health Canada, TGA, PMDA, WHO and other authorities.

A source being physically present does not make it production-effective. Governance/QA approval and applicability mapping remain mandatory.
