 Adults 15 Years of Age and Older with Asthma
 Montelukast has been evaluated in approximately 2600 adult patients 15 years
 of age and older in clinical studies. In two similarly designed, 12-week placebo-
 controlled clinical studies, the only adverse experiences reported as drug
 related in ≥ 1% of patients treated with Montelukast and at a greater incidence
 than in patients treated with placebo were abdominal pain and headache. The
 incidences of these events were not significantly different in the two treatment
 groups.
 Cumulatively, 544 patients were treated with Montelukast for at least 6
 months, 253 for one year and 21 for 2 years in clinical studies. With prolonged
 treatment, the adverse experience profile did not change.
