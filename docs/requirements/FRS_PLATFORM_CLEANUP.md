# FRS — Zero-Deviation Platform Cleanup and Capability Modernization

Document ID: FRS-PLATFORM-CLEANUP-001
Parent: URS-PLATFORM-CLEANUP-001
Status: Baseline

## 1. Functional architecture

FRS-001 Requests shall resolve an authenticated principal before resolving tenant/workspace/module context.
FRS-002 Authorization shall be evaluated by a shared AuthorizationService.
FRS-003 AuthorizationService shall read authoritative membership/entitlement state from PostgreSQL or a controlled server-side cache with safe invalidation.
FRS-004 Selected context tokens shall contain identifiers only, not self-authorizing permissions.
FRS-005 Each protected operation shall validate tenantId, workspaceId, environment, moduleKey and permission before regulated data access.

## 2. Platform interfaces

The codebase shall expose implementation-neutral interfaces:

`RelationalStore`
`VectorStore`
`SearchIndex`
`CacheStore`
`EventBus`
`ObjectStore`
`ModelGateway`
`EmbeddingService`
`RetrievalService`
`RerankingService`
`AIWorkflowOrchestrator`
`AuditService`
`EvidenceService`
`AuthorizationService`

Module domain code shall depend on these contracts, not vendor SDKs.

## 3. PostgreSQL

FRS-020 PostgreSQL shall hold regulated transactional records and governed configuration.
FRS-021 Tenant/workspace integrity shall be enforced using relational constraints where feasible.
FRS-022 Multi-table regulated writes shall use transactions.
FRS-023 Schema changes shall use ordered governed migrations.
FRS-024 Migration history shall be checksum verified.
FRS-025 Production migrations shall support controlled rehearsal/rollback planning.

## 4. Qdrant

FRS-030 Qdrant adapter shall expose tenant/workspace/module-filtered vector upsert, delete and search operations.
FRS-031 Every vector record shall carry authoritative scope metadata.
FRS-032 Retrieval shall require scope filters generated by AuthorizationService-derived context.
FRS-033 Direct user-supplied scope filters shall not be trusted as authorization.
FRS-034 Vector ingestion shall record embedding model/version and source reference.
FRS-035 Vector deletion/rebuild operations shall be auditable.

## 5. Elasticsearch

FRS-040 Elasticsearch adapter shall support full-text indexing and search projections.
FRS-041 Indexed records shall carry scope metadata.
FRS-042 Search results shall be filtered to authorized tenant/workspace/module scope.
FRS-043 Elasticsearch shall be rebuildable from authoritative sources.
FRS-044 Search index state shall never override PostgreSQL regulated state.

## 6. Redis

FRS-050 Redis adapter shall support namespaced caching, distributed locking and throttling.
FRS-051 Redis keys shall include environment and tenant/workspace context where appropriate.
FRS-052 Regulated source-of-truth data shall not exist only in Redis.
FRS-053 Cache TTL and invalidation shall be explicit.
FRS-054 Security-sensitive membership/entitlement cache shall be short lived or actively invalidated.
FRS-055 Lock ownership and expiry shall be safe against orphaned locks.

## 7. Kafka

FRS-060 Kafka adapter shall publish typed, versioned domain/platform events.
FRS-061 Event schemas shall include event id, event type/version, occurred-at timestamp and authorized scope identifiers.
FRS-062 Consumers shall be idempotent.
FRS-063 Consumer processing shall use retry/dead-letter strategy for recoverable failures.
FRS-064 Regulated mutations triggered by events shall use database transactions and create audit evidence.
FRS-065 Events shall not contain unnecessary sensitive data.
FRS-066 Event publication for regulated transactions shall use an outbox or equivalently reliable pattern where consistency requires it.

## 8. LangChain / LangGraph

FRS-070 LangChain/LangGraph shall be isolated behind AIWorkflowOrchestrator and related AI service interfaces.
FRS-071 Workflow graphs shall orchestrate tools/services but shall not define authoritative regulatory rules.
FRS-072 Prompts/policies shall be versioned.
FRS-073 Tool calls shall receive only authorized context/data.
FRS-074 Model outputs that affect regulated workflow shall be persisted with provenance/evidence as configured.
FRS-075 Model/provider changes shall not require module-domain rewrites.

## 9. Retrieval

FRS-080 RetrievalService shall support semantic retrieval.
FRS-081 RetrievalService shall support lexical/full-text retrieval.
FRS-082 RetrievalService shall support hybrid fusion where configured.
FRS-083 RerankingService shall rerank only already-authorized candidate results.
FRS-084 Retrieval evidence shall identify sources, scores/ranks and configuration required for reproducibility.
FRS-085 No retrieval result from an unauthorized scope shall be returned even if it is semantically similar.

## 10. Literature

FRS-100 Literature execution shall preserve separation between test/search utility execution and regulated workflow execution.
FRS-101 Governed search shall generate a Search Evidence Package containing configuration/query/source/timestamp/count/outcome evidence.
FRS-102 Promotion of a relevant literature result into workflow shall create traceable linkage to source evidence.
FRS-103 Literature module shall use shared platform authorization/audit/evidence/retrieval services.

## 11. Intake/Triage

FRS-110 Intake creation shall validate authorized context before persistence.
FRS-111 Intake processing shall support duplicate assessment within authorized scope.
FRS-112 Follow-up/disposition changes shall be auditable.
FRS-113 Case creation shall be transactional and idempotent against duplicate submission/retry.
FRS-114 Intake workflow failures shall surface recoverable state rather than silently discarding work.

## 12. L2A/Case Processing

FRS-120 Case processing states shall be represented by an explicit server-side state model.
FRS-121 Unauthorized state jumps shall be rejected.
FRS-122 QC/medical review/finalization actions shall require role-appropriate permissions.
FRS-123 Case version/evidence lineage shall be preserved.
FRS-124 Direct-object access shall always re-check authorized scope.

## 13. Submissions

FRS-130 Submission creation shall accept only authorized finalized case versions.
FRS-131 Submission package generation shall produce reproducible evidence.
FRS-132 Transport adapters shall be separated from submission domain rules.
FRS-133 Send operations shall use idempotency keys.
FRS-134 Transport retries shall not create duplicate submission records.
FRS-135 Receipt/acknowledgement processing shall update governed status with provenance.

## 14. Audit/evidence

FRS-140 Audit writes shall be server generated.
FRS-141 Clients shall not be able to set authoritative actor/tenant/workspace audit identity fields.
FRS-142 Audit write failure for critical regulated/security actions shall fail closed unless explicitly governed otherwise.
FRS-143 Evidence objects shall be content/version attributable.
FRS-144 Critical evidence shall be protected from ordinary update/delete operations.

## 16. Machine-enforced dependency boundaries

FRS-170 Domain modules shall not import Qdrant, Redis, Kafka, Elasticsearch or LangChain SDK packages directly.
FRS-171 Vendor SDK imports shall be limited to infrastructure adapter packages/directories.
FRS-172 Regulated domain services shall not import UI components.
FRS-173 UI routes shall not bypass domain/application authorization services for regulated writes.
FRS-174 Shared platform services shall not import module-specific domain logic.
FRS-175 Cycles between bounded contexts shall be prohibited.

## 17. Clean export

FRS-180 Export shall be generated from one immutable qualified commit.
FRS-181 A source archive checksum shall be calculated and recorded.
FRS-182 Export shall pass secret scan after archive creation.
FRS-183 Export shall contain a generated dependency/SBOM inventory if supported by the release process.
FRS-184 PROVENANCE.md shall link the new baseline to the exact original-repository commit.
